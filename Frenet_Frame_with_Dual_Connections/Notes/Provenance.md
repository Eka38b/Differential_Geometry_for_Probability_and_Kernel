# Provenance and interpretation

The archive is organized around the MathOverflow question cited in the root README. Its formulation fixes both diagonal lists, requires positive superdiagonals, and imposes the shared-tangent source condition.

Datasets 01 and 02 are exports of saved 7 November 2026 numerical campaigns. Their actual frozen matrices, recorded roots, solver-origin metadata, budgets, and exact certificate boxes are retained. Only cases satisfying the current scope are included in their counts. Legacy reports and broader-problem notes are not part of this edition.

The new shared-tangent source dataset and the alpha-connection consistency checks were performed on 16 November 2026. Their dates and environment versions are stored in the result JSON files.

The alternative zero-source algorithms, paired construction, and exact certificate verifier are adapted from the implementations used in the historical work. The source-aware common solver and replay interface provide a consistent entry point for the present question. Frozen-input replay is reproducible as a specified new run, but is not represented as bit-for-bit reconstruction of the old input-generation and search trajectories.

The raw decimal matrices are numerical approximations. Except for analytic controls and the exact rational certificates, compatibility and rank are numerical statements at stated tolerances. A saved witness is not an independent numerical discovery, a singular Jacobian is not proof of a curve, and finite root recovery is not proof of global finiteness.

The exact certificate systems define algebraic data, prove existence, preserve positive superdiagonals, and establish local isolation of selected solutions. They show different magnitudes at identical fixed data; they do not count every solution.

The local Markdown checks enforce the requested renderer conventions. They do not claim live GitHub rendering verification. The supplied MathOverflow citation is preserved exactly. The archive does not assert that the live page has been checked for later revisions or answers.
